The shape of your agreement.

Every spoke is a decision between the agency and the client. The rounder the circle, the closer you are. It does not need to be perfect to move forward, only free of deal-breakers.

How to read it

  • Dots on top of each other turn green. Agreed and complete. Fully aligned decisions form a perfect circle.
  • Dashed spoke. Being worked out. The further apart, the less aligned.
  • Dotted spoke. A decision still to be made.
  • Broken spoke. A deal-breaker. Sort it before moving on.
  • A bigger, wonky circle. Less aligned. As you pull together it tightens.
