# Milestones reference: what must be settled

The list of items the mother agent works through, artifact by artifact. `mother-agent.md` explains milestones, artifacts, settled items and who decides. This file is the list itself.

- **AR** is the agency rep. **CR** is the client rep. **PR** is client procurement.
- The names after each item say whose answer is needed. **Both** means the agency side and the client side each answer, and the two answers have to match.
- ★ marks a **core** item: it must be settled before its milestone can pass, and it is asked for first. 56 of the 134 items are core.

## PIT. Pitch strategy and creative (M0 Pitch won)

Produced by AR, reviewed by CR.

- PIT-01 ★ Which parts of the pitch are commitments and which were illustration (AR, CR)
- PIT-02 Which creative route was chosen, and what is open to change (CR)
- PIT-03 ★ Who owns the ideas shown in the pitch, and whether a pitch fee was paid (PR)
- PIT-04 ★ The team shown in the pitch is the team doing the work, with key people named (AR)
- PIT-05 The headline price or range the client heard in the pitch (AR, CR)
- PIT-06 The timeline promised in the pitch (AR, CR)
- PIT-07 Credentials and case-study claims that must be true (PR)
- PIT-08 Competing accounts or conflicts the agency holds (AR, PR)
